# Help-card content: verb + preposition (DrillVerbPrepositions)

Count: **4 rule entries** (3 new — `verb-prep-gap-fixed`, `verb-prep-gap-meaning`,
`verb-prep-case-pair`; 1 shared with `prepositions.md`, texts copied unchanged — `prep-which-fixed`), **132 items covered**
(66 preposition-gap + 66 which-case, each exactly once), **0 `UNSURE:` marks**, **0 items unplaced**.

Grouping key:

- (a) preposition-gap items: split by verb pattern. `verb-prep-gap-meaning` covers the seven verbs
  whose **two pairs are both in the drill's own data** (sich freuen, sprechen, leiden, sich bedanken,
  bestehen, sorgen/sich sorgen, kämpfen — 14 items). All other gap items use `verb-prep-gap-fixed`
  (52 items), whose rule teaches the single fixed pair. For verbs that also have a second standard
  pair not drilled here (denken an/über, erzählen von/über, berichten von/über, sich einigen
  auf/über, träumen von/über), the miss lines below are worded so they never claim the distractor is
  impossible German; the case ending of the noun in the sentence settles those where it can.
- (b) which-case items: the case is the answer, so no field before the answer names a case. Items
  whose preposition carries one fixed case on its own (für, um, gegen, mit, bei, nach, von, zu, aus —
  32 items) reuse `prep-which-fixed`. Items whose preposition is two-way on its own (auf, an, über,
  in, vor, unter — 34 items) get the new rule `verb-prep-case-pair`: the verb fixes the case.

Content decisions worth review:

- Forms-table and example sentences use verb pairs from **outside** the 66 drilled pairs
  (rechnen mit, sich streiten um, sich fürchten vor, duften nach, sich entschuldigen für/bei,
  hinweisen auf, beruhen auf). With one shared rule covering up to 52 items, any drilled pair used
  as the table example would pre-answer the answer of that one item; outside pairs teach the same
  pattern without ever leaking an answer.
- Two distractors are also grammatical standard German with another pair of the same verb:
  „kämpfen um“ (item 65) and „sich über etwas einigen“ (item 60). Their miss lines teach the drilled
  pair („für die Freiheit kämpfen“, „sich auf einen Kompromiss einigen“) instead of calling the
  distractor impossible. Same care for „erzählen über“ (37) and „berichten über“ (39), where the
  dative noun after the gap rules the distractor out by form.

**rule (pre-answer)**: Many German verbs take one fixed preposition. It belongs to the verb, not to
the meaning of the sentence, so learn each verb with its preposition as one unit.

**first_exposure_rule**: none — the pattern needs no case or gender name (its items include both
cases, so no single name would be true for all of them).

  - ✗ *bei*: bei belongs to *helfen bei*. The illness pair is *leiden an*.

  - ✗ *an*: *leiden an* is for an illness you suffer from. Hitze is a condition around you, so *leiden unter* (unter der Hitze).

  - ✗ *bei*: bei belongs to *helfen bei*. The condition pair is *leiden unter*.

  - ✗ *um*: um belongs to *bitten um*. *bedanken* pairs with für for a thing, bei for a person.
- *Das Team besteht ___ fünf erfahrenen Entwicklern.* (answer: aus)
  - ✗ *von*: von belongs to *träumen von*. Saying what something is made of is *bestehen aus* (aus fünf Entwicklern).
  - ✗ *an*: an belongs to *teilnehmen an*. The made-of pair is *bestehen aus*.
  - ✗ *in*: *bestehen in* says what something consists in, as an idea (Das Problem besteht in …). The members a team is made of take *bestehen aus*.
- *Er besteht stur ___ seinem vertraglichen Recht.* (answer: auf)
  - ✗ *an*: an belongs to *teilnehmen an*. Insisting on something is *bestehen auf* (auf seinem Recht).
  - ✗ *in*: in belongs to *sich verlieben in*. *bestehen* pairs with auf here.
  - ✗ *zu*: zu belongs to *einladen zu*. *bestehen* has two pairs: auf for insisting, aus for what something is made of.
- *Die Eltern sorgen sich ___ die Zukunft ihrer Kinder.* (answer: um)
  - ✗ *für*: *sorgen für* is providing something. Worrying about something is *sich sorgen um* (um die Zukunft).
  - ✗ *über*: über belongs to *sich ärgern über*. The worry pair is *sich sorgen um*.
  - ✗ *an*: an belongs to *denken an*. *sorgen* has two pairs: *sich sorgen um* for worry, *sorgen für* for providing.
- *Gute Musik sorgt immer ___ beste Stimmung.* (answer: für)
  - ✗ *um*: *sich sorgen um* is worrying about something. Providing something is *sorgen für* (für beste Stimmung).
  - ✗ *auf*: auf belongs to *warten auf*. *sorgen* pairs with für here.
  - ✗ *an*: an belongs to *denken an*. *sorgen* has two pairs: für for providing, *sich um* for worrying.
- *Die Menschen kämpfen mutig ___ ihre Freiheit.* (answer: für)
  - ✗ *um*: *kämpfen um* is fighting to win something. Standing up for a value like freedom is *kämpfen für* (für ihre Freiheit).
  - ✗ *gegen*: gegen means fighting against something, the opposite direction. Here they fight on freedom's side, so *kämpfen für*.
  - ✗ *auf*: auf belongs to *warten auf*. *kämpfen* pairs with für or gegen.
- *Der Arzt kämpft jeden Tag ___ die Krankheit.* (answer: gegen)
  - ✗ *für*: *kämpfen für* is fighting on something's side. Die Krankheit is the opponent here, so *kämpfen gegen* (gegen die Krankheit).
  - ✗ *wider*: wider also means against, but it lives in fixed expressions. The everyday pair is *kämpfen gegen*.
  - ✗ *um*: *kämpfen um* is fighting to win something. Fighting an opponent is *kämpfen gegen*.
