Drill round — recognition ladder (#639)

Mock only, no screen work yet. One round = 5–8 items, ≤ ~12 interactions, 2–3 min. Steps below show the ladder: flashcard → tap → match / true-false / odd-one-out / word-bank / listen-and-tap → typed, with inline corrections and an "Another 5" summary. Listen-and-tap is planned, not live (#532). Tokens mirror the app's light/dark themes.

Interleaving in this demo round (mixed: Articles · Prepositions · Verb + preposition):
1 flashcard (Verb + prep · new) → 2 tap (Articles) → 3 true-false (Verb + prep) → 4 odd one out (Verb + preposition — a label only; steps 3 and 4 may share a type, the no-repeat rule is about formats) → 5 match (Articles) → 6 word bank (Prepositions) → 7 listen-and-tap (Verb + prep · planned #532) → 8 typed (Articles · graduation) → summary. No two consecutive questions share a format, and every step is a different item. Tap a wrong answer on step 2 to see the inline correction. The optional speed finale is not shown.
A flashcard item's first tap comes later in the same round or in the next one — step 1's item (sich freuen über) is tapped in the next round, which is why the demo shows no tap for it here.
Interactions on the shown path: 11 — flashcard 1 · tap + correction 2 · true-false 1 · odd one out 1 · match 3 · word bank 1 · listen 1 · typed 1 — inside the "at most about 12" budget. A whole word-bank or listen-and-tap answer counts as one interaction, and the typed step's close-spelling retry is part of the same interaction (see the doc, Rounds).
