Drill screen motion (#640)

Mock only, no screen work yet. The subject is the motion: a slide/fade transition between questions (no hard re-bind), a feedback micro-animation on right and wrong, and an entrance animation on the round summary. Play the round end to end — wrong answers on purpose show the inline correction. Content follows the #639 round model (mixed formats, inline corrections); look and tokens are shared with drills-round-mock.html.

Motion principles being judged (durations in the CSS):
· Question change: old card fades out left 140ms, new card slides in from the right 220ms, easing cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1) — sequential, never a hard swap.
· Only the question card animates; the header, progress and segment bar stay put.
· Right: tile settles to sage and a check mark pops 180ms; auto-advance after ~500ms.
· Wrong: rose flash + shake 320ms, other tiles dim, the inline correction slides up 200ms with the one-line rule — one tap on Continue.
· Near miss (close spelling): no shake, no rose — the input border pulses sand once, one retry.
· Summary entrance: counts pop, rows rise in a 50ms stagger, buttons come last.
· Reduced motion (system setting or the Motion pill): all transforms removed, opacity-only fades.
