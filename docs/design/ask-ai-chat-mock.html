✗ You picked den. den is masculine accusative; Fenster is neuter, so das.
The sentence isn’t about standing — it’s about putting yourself there. Er stellt sich = he positions himself: he walks over and plants himself next to the window. Movement into a place with a two-way preposition takes the accusative, and das Fenster stays das in the accusative.
So would “Er steht neben dem Fenster” also be right?
Yes — but it means something different. stehen is the “be standing” verb, a fixed position: Wo? → Dativ, so dem. The pair is stellen (put — Akk) vs stehen (be — Dat). Same pattern: legen / liegen, setzen / sitzen.
