The glass water is the home prototype's: drops sit, slip, merge and leave a short wet sheen. New for rain looks: a few drops hit the glass. Each hit makes a thin ring and two to four small beads that slide downwind and fade. Wind (0 to 100, default 30) sets how far the beads slide; each look lets only part of it through. Hits mostly land away from the clock, date and app names. The look buttons are for this preview only. Theme, pause and reduced motion are mock tools; the app follows the system.
